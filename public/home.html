Big Pace Ledger

Appointment and sales-pace tracking for our insurance advisory team.

Big Pace Ledger (PaceLedger) is an internal tool used by advisors, managers, and administrators to log client and recruiting appointments, track weekly pace against team standards, manage follow-ups and policy requirements, and coordinate prospecting activity — all in one place.

What it does

  • Logs appointments with automatic Zoom meeting creation and optional Google Calendar sync
  • Tracks weekly appointment-setting pace against team targets
  • Manages follow-ups, policy requirements, and prospecting pipelines
  • Gives managers and administrators oversight of their team's activity and progress toward promotion milestones

Big Pace Ledger is built for internal use by our own organization — accounts are created by and for members of our team, not the general public.
