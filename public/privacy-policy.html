PaceLedger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

About this document: Big Pace Ledger ("PaceLedger", big-pace-ledger.com) is an appointment, prospecting and sales-pace tracking tool used by insurance advisors, managers and administrators within our organization. It is not a public consumer product — accounts are created by, and for, members of our team. This policy explains what PaceLedger collects, how it is used, and how data from Google and Zoom is handled.

Information we collect

From you, when you have an account

  • Account details: your name, email address, role, and who you report to. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a one-way hash — we never see your actual password.
  • Prospects: names, an optional email address, where the prospect came from, and notes you add while prospecting.
  • Appointments: client or recruit names, an optional client email address, presenters, dates and times, meeting links, notes, sale or recruit outcomes and related premium figures, plus the email addresses you choose to send a meeting invitation to.
  • Follow-ups: follow-up notes you add to past appointments.
  • Planning and team data: your Business Plan and Marketing Plan answers, milestones, trainings, availability, and documents your organization uploads.
  • First 30 days checklist: which steps of the new-advisor checklist you've completed (for example "Business Plan filled in" or "calendar connected", as yes or no) and when you last opened PaceLedger. Your manager can see this so they know when to help. It doesn't include the contents of your Business Plan or calendar.

About clients and prospects (people who don't have an account)

Advisors enter the names and, optionally, email addresses of the clients and prospects they work with. An advisor can also send a client a private client intake link. If the client chooses to fill it out, the form collects the financial-planning information the client provides — for example date of birth, state, marital and tax-filing status, retirement goals, income, expenses, savings and coverage — and makes it available to that advisor (and the advisor's managers) to prepare for their meeting. Clients reach these forms only through the link their advisor sends them.

Invitations

PaceLedger accounts are by invitation only. A team member creates a one-time invitation link that decides who the new person will report to. The link holds only a random code. Before signing up, the person opening it sees only their own name (if the inviter entered it) and the names of the team they're joining. Each link works once and expires after 30 days.

How we use this information

We use this information only to operate PaceLedger's features: tracking appointment-setting pace against team standards, prospecting, scheduling and following up on appointments, sending meeting invitations you ask us to send, and reporting sales and recruiting activity to the appropriate managers and administrators. We do not sell or rent any information, we do not use it for advertising, and the app contains no advertising.

Who can see your information

Access follows your organization's reporting structure:

  • You can always see your own data.
  • Your managers above you in the reporting structure can see your appointments, prospects, follow-ups, client intake forms and pace, so they can coach and support you.
  • Super administrators can see data across the organization, for oversight.

These rules are enforced by security rules inside the database itself, not just by the app's screens.

Google Calendar integration

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, PaceLedger requests a single permission: see, create, change and delete events on Google calendars you own (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.owned scope). We only ever use your primary calendar, and we don't request access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive, or any other Google data.

What we do with this access

  • Show your schedule in one place. On PaceLedger's Calendar tab we read your Google Calendar events for the dates you're viewing and display them alongside your PaceLedger appointments. These events are fetched when you view the calendar and shown only to you; we do not store copies of them.
  • Add appointments to your calendar. When you log an appointment in PaceLedger, we create a matching event on your Google Calendar with the appointment's title, time and Zoom meeting link.
  • Send meeting invitations you ask for. If you add invitees to an appointment (for example your client's email address, your own, or a co-presenter's), we add those email addresses to the event as attendees, and Google sends them the standard calendar invitation from your calendar. We add only the addresses you enter for that appointment.
  • Managers hosting a meeting. If you are a manager or administrator who has connected both Zoom and Google Calendar, and an advisor chooses your Zoom account to host an appointment's meeting, PaceLedger adds that appointment (title, time and Zoom link) to your Google Calendar too, so you know about the meeting you're hosting. Invitations are never sent from a manager's calendar.

What we store, and for how long

We store your Google access and refresh tokens and your connected calendar's address (shown to you as "Connected as …"). Tokens are stored in our database, cannot be read by the app in your browser (only by our server-side functions), and are used only to make the calendar requests described above on your behalf. Events PaceLedger creates are not changed afterwards: if you reschedule an appointment, update the Google event yourself or send an updated invitation from PaceLedger (see below).

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time with the Disconnect button on PaceLedger's Calendar tab. That deletes our stored tokens immediately and asks Google to revoke PaceLedger's access. You can also remove PaceLedger from your Google Account's third-party access page; we then delete our stored tokens the next time PaceLedger tries to use them. Events PaceLedger already created stay on your Google Calendar as ordinary events, which you can edit or delete in Google Calendar.

Who we share Google user data with

Google user data means the calendar events we read from your Google Calendar, your connected calendar's address, and the access tokens Google gives us. We share it only as follows:

  • Our service providers, only to run the features above. Supabase hosts our database and server functions: it stores your Google tokens and connected calendar address, and runs the functions that send requests to Google Calendar for you. Cloudflare hosts the PaceLedger website, so calendar data passes through its network (encrypted) on its way to your screen; it doesn't store it. Both act on our behalf under their terms and may not use the data for anything else.
  • People you choose to invite. When you add invitees to an appointment, Google sends them a calendar invitation from your calendar with the event's title, time and meeting link. Only the addresses you enter receive it.
  • When the law requires it, or as part of a merger or acquisition, with notice to you.

We do not share Google user data with your managers or other PaceLedger users (Google Calendar events are shown only to you), with Zoom, with our email provider, or with advertisers or data brokers, and we never sell it.

Limited Use

PaceLedger's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use Google Calendar data only to provide the user-facing calendar features described above.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others, except as described in "Who we share Google user data with" above: to provide those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • No person reads your Google Calendar data unless you give us permission to (for example, to help with a support request), it is needed for security purposes or to comply with the law, or it is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Zoom integration

If you choose to connect your Zoom account, PaceLedger requests permission to create meetings on your behalf (the meeting:write:meeting scope). When you log an appointment, we create a unique Zoom meeting and attach the join link to that appointment. We don't access any other Zoom data, such as recordings, contacts or account settings. You can disconnect Zoom at any time from within PaceLedger, or from your Zoom account's connected-apps settings; either removes our access and our stored copy of your token.

Meeting invitation emails

When an invitation can't come from your Google Calendar — because you haven't connected it, the Google step didn't go through, or you're sending an updated invitation after changing an appointment — PaceLedger can email the meeting details to the invitees you enter, through our email delivery provider, Resend. Each email goes only to the addresses you entered for that appointment, comes with a calendar file attached, and lists you as the reply-to address. Sending is rate-limited to prevent misuse.

iPhone and Android apps

The PaceLedger iPhone and Android apps show the same account and data as the website. If you turn on appointment reminders, the reminders are scheduled on your phone from your own appointments; nothing extra is sent to us. When you share a link or export a spreadsheet, it goes only where you choose in your phone's share sheet. You can turn reminders off at any time in your phone's Settings. The apps contain no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication, database and server-side functions.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting.
  • Google and Zoom — only if you connect them, as described above.
  • Resend — delivery of invitation emails you ask us to send.

If a manager adds a Calendly link to their profile, clicking it takes you to Calendly's own site, governed by Calendly's privacy policy.

Data storage and security

Data is stored in a hosted PostgreSQL database (Supabase) and protected by database-level security rules that enforce the access described above. All connections use HTTPS encryption, and responses that contain personal or token data are marked so browsers and proxies don't cache them.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your own account at any time, on the website or in the app: select your name at the top right, then Your account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your login and the records you own (appointments, prospects, notes, business plan, intake forms you sent, and your Google and Zoom connections). A super administrator can also remove an account. Google and Zoom tokens are deleted as soon as you disconnect. To ask for your data, or a client's data, to be deleted, contact us at the address below, or see how to delete your account.

Children's privacy

PaceLedger is a workplace tool for adult employees and contractors of our organization. It is not directed at, and should not be used by, children.
