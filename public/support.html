PaceLedger — Support

PaceLedger is an internal appointment and sales-pace tracking tool used by advisors, managers, and administrators within our organization.

Need help?

If you're a PaceLedger user and run into an issue — with logging appointments, your Zoom or Google Calendar connection, or anything else — reach out to your organization's PaceLedger administrator, who can escalate as needed.

Zoom or Google Calendar connection issues

You can disconnect and reconnect either integration at any time from within the app (in the Calendar tab for Google, or My Appointments for Zoom) — this resolves most connection issues on its own.
