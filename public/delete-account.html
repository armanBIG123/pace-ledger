PaceLedger — Delete your account

PaceLedger (Big Pace Ledger) by Big Insurance Network

You can permanently delete your PaceLedger account and its data yourself, at any time, in the app or on the website. You don't need to reinstall anything.

Delete it yourself

  1. Open the PaceLedger app (iPhone or Android), or go to big-pace-ledger.com and log in.
  2. Tap or click your name at the top right.
  3. Choose Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

That's it: your account is deleted straight away and you're logged out.

Or ask us to delete it

If you can't log in, email arman@biginsurancenetwork.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my PaceLedger account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your login, name and email address.
  • Everything you've added: appointments, prospects, follow-up notes, your Business Plan, client intake forms you sent, and coaching notes about you.
  • Your Google Calendar and Zoom connections (we also ask Google and Zoom to revoke PaceLedger's access).

What may be kept

  • Entries in your organization's admin activity log that mention your name (for example, "an admin changed your team"), as a record of those changes.
  • Training documents you uploaded for your team, if you're an administrator: they stay available to the team.

Backups held by our database provider expire on their normal schedule.

If someone reports to you, an administrator will assign them a new manager after your account is deleted.
