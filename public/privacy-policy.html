PaceLedger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

About this document: PaceLedger is an internal appointment and sales-pace tracking tool used by advisors, managers, and administrators within our organization. It is not a public consumer product — accounts are created by, and for, members of our team.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored securely as a one-way hash — we never see or store your actual password). As you use the app, it stores the appointment details you enter: client or recruit names, presenters, dates and times, notes, sale or recruit outcomes, and related premium figures.

How we use this information

This information is used solely to operate PaceLedger's core functions: tracking appointment-setting pace against team standards, coordinating follow-ups, and reporting sales and recruiting activity to the appropriate managers and administrators. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes, and the app contains no advertising.

Who can see your information

Access follows your organization's reporting structure:

  • You can always see your own appointments and data.
  • If you're an advisor, your assigned manager can see your logged appointments and pace.
  • Super administrators can see all appointments and users across the organization, for oversight purposes.

Data storage and security

Data is stored using Supabase (a hosted PostgreSQL database provider) and protected by database-level security rules that enforce the access described above. All connections to the app use HTTPS encryption.

Third-party services

PaceLedger uses Supabase for authentication and data storage, and Cloudflare for hosting. If a manager or administrator adds a Calendly link to their profile, clicking that link takes you to Calendly's own site, governed by Calendly's own privacy policy — we don't control or receive data from that separately.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account remains active. A super administrator can remove your account at any time, which removes your profile and prevents further access; associated appointment history may be retained for organizational record-keeping.

Children's privacy

PaceLedger is a workplace tool intended for adult employees and contractors of our organization. It is not directed at, and should not be used by, children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to your organization's PaceLedger administrator.
